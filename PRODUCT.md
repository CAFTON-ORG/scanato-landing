# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Next.js App Router app with TypeScript, Tailwind v4, and shadcn/ui (base-luma). Delegated by the user: keep this stack.

## Users

**Inferred (user asked to proceed without an interview):** restaurant, cafe, and retail operators who take orders at the table or counter and also run stock and checkout. Guests are a secondary audience who scan a QR code to order.

## Product Purpose

Scanato is a scan-to-order product for hospitality and retail. Guests scan, browse, and order. Staff run POS and inventory from the same system. Success is faster ordering with fewer mistakes between floor, kitchen, and stock.

## Positioning

**Inferred:** one scan opens ordering, checkout, and inventory together, so the floor, till, and stockroom are not three disconnected tools.

## Operating Context

Guests use a phone at the table. Staff use a till or back-office screen. Physical objects in the scene: QR table tents, kitchen tickets, receipts, barcode labels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed by the user for this first landing: scan-to-order, and the product may also cover ecommerce, POS, and inventory.

Undecided: pricing, live product URL, customers, metrics, legal copy, and which modules ship first.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Scanato. User asked for a simple first landing and to decide app structure. Use shadcn/ui. Design skills: design-taste-frontend, impeccable, ui-ux-pro-max.

## Evidence on Hand

No logo, photography, testimonials, customers, or benchmarks. Do not invent commercial claims. Demonstration UI on the landing is synthetic and labeled.

## Product Principles

1. Show the scan-to-order mechanism before listing modules.
2. Speak to operators first; guests are how the product works, not the buyer.
3. Keep claims within confirmed capabilities: order, POS, inventory, ecommerce.
4. Prefer one clear action: request a demo.
5. Do not fabricate social proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard was set. Default to WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.
